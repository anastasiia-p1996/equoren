Privacy notice

How we handle the information you send us.

This notice explains what happens to the details you submit through the contact form on this website, or send to us by email. It is written in plain language rather than legal formulas.

Last updated 22 August 2026

Who is responsible

Equoren is an early-stage, founder-led project. It is not yet an incorporated company. Responsibility for the information described here rests with the individual operating Equoren, reachable at hello@equoren.com. If and when Equoren is formally incorporated, this notice will be updated with the registered entity's details.

What we collect

Only what you choose to type into the contact form:

  • Your name
  • Your work email address
  • Your company name, if you provide it
  • What you would like to improve, and any message you add

We do not ask for, and do not want, sensitive information. Please don't include confidential business data, credentials or personal details of your staff in a first message.

Why we use it

To read your inquiry, reply to it, and assess whether we can help with the problem you describe. Nothing else. We do not add you to a mailing list, we do not sell or share your details for marketing, and we do not use them for automated decision-making or profiling.

Services that process it for us

Three third parties are involved in running this site. Each holds data on our behalf under its own privacy terms, and their servers may be located outside your country.

Web3Forms
Delivers contact form submissions to our inbox.
Cloudflare
Hosts the site, routes email sent to our address, and provides visitor statistics. The analytics are cookie-free and aggregated — they do not identify individual visitors, which is why this site shows no cookie banner.
Google Fonts
Serves the typefaces used on this page. Loading them involves a request to Google's servers.

How long we keep it

For as long as the conversation is relevant, and no longer than 24 months after our last exchange, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or we have agreed to work together.

Your choices

Write to hello@equoren.com and we will act on any of the following, normally within 30 days:

  • Ask what we hold about you, and get a copy
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Have it deleted
  • Object to us contacting you further

If you are in the EU, the UK or another region with equivalent rules, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this notice

Equoren is at an early stage, so how we work will change. When this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.
